----------------------------------------------------------------------
KEY TAKEAWAYS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

1. DL can capture nonlinear gene interactions that linear models miss
2. Deeper is NOT always better -- medium (2-layer) often wins on small data
3. Bottleneck architectures learn compressed representations (like autoencoders)
4. Wide networks risk overfitting on limited genomic samples
5. 500 input features with 350 noise genes -- DL must learn to ignore noise
6. Batch normalization is critical for deep genomic networks
7. Dropout prevents co-adaptation -- essential when features >> samples
8. This is the FOUNDATION for Days 36+ (RNA folding, multi-omics, etc.)
