FIRST TRANSFER LEARNING PROJECT!

3 strategies compared: scratch | frozen | finetune

Strategy  Accuracy  F1 (weighted)  F1 (macro)  Time (s)
 scratch    1.0000         1.0000      1.0000  582.3657
finetune    1.0000         1.0000      1.0000  466.8131
  frozen    0.9924         0.9924      0.9920  466.4414

KEY TAKEAWAYS:
1. Pretrained ResNet (finetune) usually beats training from scratch
2. Even frozen features (trained on ImageNet) transfer to medical images
3. Fine-tuning with low LR preserves pretrained knowledge while adapting
4. Transfer learning needs LESS data and FEWER epochs to converge
5. ImageNet normalization (mean/std) is required for pretrained models
6. Per-class analysis shows transfer helps rare classes most
7. This is the foundation for Days 24-28 (VGG, DenseNet, Inception)
