KEY TAKEAWAYS:

2. Dice loss handles class imbalance (tiny tumor vs large background)
3. Dice score > 0.7 is clinically useful for tumor segmentation
4. False negatives (missed tumor) are worse than false positives
5. The encoder-decoder architecture generalizes to many medical imaging tasks
