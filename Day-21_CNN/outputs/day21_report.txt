KEY TAKEAWAYS:

2. Data augmentation (flip, rotate, translate, jitter) prevents overfitting
3. Grayscale input (1 channel) is sufficient for X-ray classification
4. In pneumonia detection: FN (missed) is worse than FP (false alarm)
5. Recall is the priority metric for screening applications
6. AdaptiveAvgPool makes the CNN flexible to different input sizes
7. Dropout(0.5) is aggressive but needed for small medical datasets
